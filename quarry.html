Stock Added
What is sitting in the stockpiles right now, pit by pit.
On hand = Opening balance + Produced (Crushing) − Sold (Sales) + Adjustments
  • Produced comes straight off the Crushing Tracking tab (loads to crusher × tons/load). Sold comes straight off the Sales Tracking tab. Both update the moment you edit those tabs — nothing here is typed twice.
  • Stock Added picks the production basis. Final screen tons (the default) applies each pit's jaws → screen loss % from Analytics, so only sellable product is added to stock; tons to crusher uses the raw crushed tonnage. Pits with no loss % entered are treated the same either way.
  • Opening balance is the stockpile that already existed before you started tracking — enter it per pit below. Production and sales dated on or before its as-of date are skipped so nothing is counted twice.
  • Adjustments are everything the two tracking tabs can't see: physical-count corrections, waste hauled off, material used internally, transfers between pits. Enter tons as a negative number to take material out.
  • Every balance splits by product as well as by pit — see On Hand by Product below. Crushing runs booked before the Product column existed have nothing to tag them with, so their tons sit in Untagged until you set a Product on those rows.
  • Location, Product and the search box narrow every figure on this tab, so filtering to one material shows that material's balance — days of supply included. Status filters the by-product table to what's low, oversold, or empty.

Break-Even Analysis
Loading…

How many tons must each pit sell to stop losing money?
Break-even tons / mo = Monthly fixed costs ÷ (Avg price/ton − Variable cost/ton)
  • Avg price/ton and variable cost/ton are pulled automatically from your Sales and Crushing entries for the selected year. Variable cost = crushing labor & fuel ÷ tons crushed (daily tracking is not included).
  • Royalty (per pit, in the table below) comes off the price, lowering contribution. Click a pit's Royalty % cell to add its owners — each pit can stack several (e.g., mineral + surface + energies), and each owner is the greater of its % of the sale or a $/ton floor. The cell shows the combined rate; contribution and break-even update automatically. Royalty applies only to material you flag as royalty-bearing — open Manage Lists → Product and check the rock products that carry a royalty; sales of unchecked products (fill, millings, etc.) are excluded, so contribution isn't over-deducted.
  • Contribution/ton = price − variable cost — what each ton puts toward fixed costs. If it's $0 or less, you lose money on every ton and there's no break-even.
  • Monthly fixed costs are the bills you pay no matter how much you crush: equipment payments, insurance, permits/royalties, salaried staff, taxes. Enter them per month in the Month-by-Month table below — pick a pit and type each month's figure. This column shows each location's average monthly fixed; All Pits is the total across pits (your full company-wide monthly fixed), and the executive dashboard mirrors it. Entries save automatically.

Bulk Upload Daily Tracking
Upload a CSV with these columns (header row required): Date, Location, Employee, Equipment, Task, Hours, Rate, Fuel / Gallons, PPG. Required: Date, Employee, Hours. Date may be YYYY-MM-DD or M/D/YYYY. Labor / fuel / total cost columns are included in the downloaded template for layout but are recomputed on import.
Bulk Upload Crushing Tracking
Upload a CSV with these columns (header row required): Date, Location, Employee, Hourly Rate, Hours, Fuel Gallons, $/Gal, Loads to Crusher, Tons / Load, Hours Crushing, Comments, Product. Required: Date, Employee, Hours. Product is what the run was making — leave it blank and the tons land in Inventory's Untagged bucket. $/Gal is the pump price per gallon (e.g. 4.50), not the day's fuel bill — that is computed as gallons × $/Gal. A sheet built against the older template, where this column was headed Fuel Cost, still imports into it. The downloaded template also includes the computed columns (Total Payroll, Fuel Cost, Estimated Tons, Tons/Hour, Total Cost, Cost/Ton) so column positions line up with the tracker — values in those columns are recomputed on import.
Bulk Upload Sales Tracking
Upload a CSV with these columns (header row required): Date, Location, Employee, Customer, Product, Tons, Payment, Price / Ton. Required: Date, Customer, Product, Tons, Price / Ton. Payment must be Cash or Credit. Total sales is computed automatically.
