Manage Lists
Bulk Upload Daily Tracking
Upload a CSV with these columns (header row required): Date, Location, Employee, Equipment, Task, Hours, Rate, Fuel / Gallons, PPG. Required: Date, Employee, Hours. Date may be YYYY-MM-DD or M/D/YYYY. Labor / fuel / total cost columns are included in the downloaded template for layout but are recomputed on import.
Bulk Upload Crushing Tracking
Upload a CSV with these columns (header row required): Date, Location, Employee, Hourly Rate, Hours, Fuel Gallons, Fuel Cost, Loads to Crusher, Tons / Load, Hours Crushing, Comments. Required: Date, Employee, Hours. The downloaded template also includes the computed columns (Total Payroll, Total Fuel, Estimated Tons, Tons/Hour, Total Cost, Cost/Ton) so column positions line up with the tracker — values in those columns are recomputed on import.
Bulk Upload Sales Tracking
Upload a CSV with these columns (header row required): Date, Location, Employee, Customer, Product, Tons, Payment, Price / Ton. Required: Date, Customer, Product, Tons, Price / Ton. Payment must be Cash or Credit. Total sales is computed automatically.
